## CONFIRMATION OF FEDERAL LANDS PRIORITY ACCESS DESIGNATION

This letter confirms that **Sovereign AI Corridor Inc.** has been designated under the **Federal Lands Priority Access** framework for the following project:

**Project Name**: Sovereign AI Data Corridor  
**Project Location**: Nanticoke Crown Land, Thorold, Ontario (PIN 382000065)  
**Project Type**: AI Data Centre and Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)  
**Anticipated Capacity**: 200 MW

---

## DESIGNATION RATIONALE

This designation is granted based on the project's alignment with the following federal priorities:

1. **Digital Infrastructure**: Development of sovereign AI computing capacity
2. **Strategic Economic Interest**: Support for Canada's AI leadership
3. **Energy Security**: Grid-scale battery storage supporting Ontario's electricity system
4. **Regional Development**: Economic growth in the Niagara Region

---

## PRIORITY ACCESS BENEFITS

Under this designation, the Proponent receives:

- **Expedited processing** for Crown land access applications
- **Priority consideration** for infrastructure coordination
- **Federal support** for intergovernmental coordination
