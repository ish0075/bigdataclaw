## RECITALS

**WHEREAS** the Crown in right of Ontario holds fee simple title to certain lands described herein (the "Site"), which are subject to existing Hydro One Networks Inc. 230 kV transmission easements as shown in the Parcel Register;

**WHEREAS** the Site is situated within or adjacent to an existing Hydro One 230 kV transmission corridor, and Hydro One Transmission Services personnel have confirmed that the 230 kV transmission circuit in the vicinity of the Site is energized and operational;

**WHEREAS** the Licensee proposes to conduct exploratory testing and environmental assessment for a sovereign AI data centre and BESS facility, with anticipated interconnection at 230 kV or higher to the existing Hydro One transmission corridor;

**WHEREAS** the Licensee has secured designation from Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) for Federal Lands Priority Access for the project;

**WHEREAS** the parties desire to enter into an exploratory licence on terms that defer major financial commitments and construction activities until Hydro One Networks Inc. issues a formal interconnection feasibility confirmation for a 200 MW-scale data centre load;

**NOW THEREFORE** in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 – DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Connection Confirmation Date"** means the date on which Hydro One Networks Inc. issues written confirmation to the Licensee confirming:

(a) Available electrical capacity of not less than 200 MW at the Site;  
(b) A feasible interconnection pathway at 230 kV or higher;  
(c) An estimated timeline for execution of a Connection Agreement; and  
(d) That no major Hydro-led transmission infrastructure upgrades are required for the proposed 200 MW-scale interconnection.

**1.2 "Effective Date"** means the date of this Agreement.

**1.3 "Exploratory Activities"** means environmental testing, geotechnical investigation, surveys, borings, and other non-constructive activities necessary to assess site suitability for data centre and BESS development.

**1.4 "Site"** means the lands described in Schedule A hereto.

**1.5 "Existing 230 kV Easements"** means the Hydro One Networks Inc. registered easements for 230 kV transmission facilities over the Site, as shown in the Parcel Register and attached as Exhibit C.

---

## ARTICLE 2 – GRANT OF LICENCE

**2.1** The Crown hereby grants to the Licensee a **non-exclusive** exploratory licence to enter upon and use the Site strictly for Exploratory Activities only.

*[Note: If IO accepts exclusivity, change to: "exclusive exploratory licence"]*

**2.2** This licence does not authorize:

(a) Construction of permanent buildings or structures;  
(b) Alteration of grade or topography beyond minimal, non-permanent surveys or test borings;  
(c) Installation of permanent infrastructure;  
(d) Discharge of pollutants or hazardous materials not otherwise permitted by law.

**2.3** The Licensee shall not assign, sublet, or transfer this licence without prior written consent of the Crown.

**2.4 Exclusivity Statement:**

During the Initial Term and any Renewal Term, the Crown shall not grant any competing Crown-land use rights, licences, easements, or permits on the Site that would materially conflict with the Licensee's right to pursue a long-term AI data centre and BESS facility, **subject to and following formal confirmation of Hydro One interconnection feasibility for at least 200 MW at 230 kV or higher**.

---

## ARTICLE 3 – TERM

**3.1** The term of this licence shall be **twelve (12) months** from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").

**3.2** The Licensee may renew this licence for **one (1) additional twelve (12) month term** upon written notice to the Crown at least sixty (60) days prior to expiry, provided:

(a) The Licensee is not in default of any material obligation under this Agreement;  
(b) The Crown has not received a higher-priority Crown use proposal for the Site (e.g., critical public infrastructure);  
(c) The parties are negotiating in good faith toward a long-term lease if Hydro One issues a formal interconnection feasibility confirmation.

---

## ARTICLE 4 – RENT AND PAYMENTS

**4.1 ANNUAL RENT:** The Licensee shall pay rent of **ONE DOLLAR ($1.00)** per annum, payable within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date.

**4.2 SECURITY DEPOSIT:**

The Licensee's obligation to pay a security deposit of **[AMOUNT TBD]** is **expressly conditioned upon** the Licensee receiving written interconnection feasibility confirmation from Hydro One Networks Inc. confirming at least 200 MW of available capacity at 230 kV or higher at the Site.

Upon such confirmation, the Licensee shall pay the security deposit within thirty (30) days.

**4.3 ADDITIONAL FEES:** During the term of this licence, the Licensee shall pay:

(a) Any minimal Crown-land administration or rental fee required by the applicable Crown land rental policy, if applicable;  
(b) Property taxes pro-rated share, due annually;  
(c) Insurance as required under Article 6;  
(d) Utilities for any metered services consumed.

**4.4 Condition Precedent** (focus on construction / major payments):

The Licensee's obligation to pay any amounts other than the $1.00 annual rent, or to commence construction or permanent works on the Site, is **expressly conditioned upon** the Licensee's receipt of the Hydro One Interconnection Confirmation (as defined in Exhibit B).

In the absence of such confirmation, the Licensee shall only proceed with non-constructive Exploratory Activities.

---

## ARTICLE 5 – USE RESTRICTIONS (Easement-Aware)

**5.1** The Licensee shall use the Site solely for Exploratory Activities related to assessment of AI data centre and BESS development feasibility, and in full compliance with existing Hydro One 230 kV Easements and the Crown land rental policy.

**5.2** The Licensee shall:

(a) Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and permits;  
(b) Not interfere with Hydro One's operation, maintenance, or safety rights under the Existing 230 kV Easements;  
(c) Restore the Site to original condition upon termination, except for minor survey markings or temporary fencing;  
(d) Provide the Crown with access for inspection upon reasonable notice.

**5.3 Prohibited activities** include:

(a) Mining, excavation, or removal of natural resources beyond minimal geotechnical or environmental-site-assessment borings;  
(b) Discharge of contaminants or hazardous materials;  
(c) Activities affecting species at risk or critical habitat;  
(d) Overnight accommodation or permanent residential use.

---

## ARTICLE 6 – INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

**6.1** The Licensee shall maintain:

(a) **Commercial general liability insurance**: $5,000,000 per occurrence;  
(b) **Environmental liability insurance**: $10,000,000 per occurrence;  
(c) **Automobile liability**: $2,000,000 per occurrence.

**6.2** The Licensee shall indemnify and hold harmless the Crown from any claims arising from the Licensee's use of the Site, except to the extent caused by the Crown's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

---

## ARTICLE 7 – DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

**7.1** Either party may terminate this Agreement upon material breach by the other party, provided ninety (90) days written notice and opportunity to cure.

**7.2** The Crown may terminate immediately for:

(a) Abandonment of the Site;  
(b) Use contrary to this Agreement;  
(c) Failure to maintain required insurance.

---

## ARTICLE 8 – GENERAL PROVISIONS

**8.1 Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Ontario.

**8.2 Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

**8.3 Amendments:** Amendments must be in writing signed by both parties.

**8.4 Notices:** All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth above.

---

## ARTICLE 9 – CONVERSION TO LONG-TERM LEASE

**9.1** If the Licensee receives the Hydro One Interconnection Confirmation (as defined in Exhibit B) during the term of this licence, the parties shall negotiate in good faith to convert this licence to a long-term lease within **ninety (90) days**.

**9.2** The long-term lease shall include, but not be limited to:

(a) **Term**: Twenty (20) years with up to two (2) ten-year renewal options;  
(b) **Rent**: Crown land industrial rate (currently $3,000–$5,000 per acre per year);  
(c) **Permitted use**: AI data centre, BESS, and ancillary facilities, subject to Hydro One easement and safety requirements;  
(d) **Performance guarantees**: Construction milestones, security deposit, and environmental-compliance covenants.

**9.3** If the parties cannot agree on the long-term lease terms within ninety (90) days, this licence may be extended by mutual written agreement or may terminate at the Crown's option, provided the Licensee is in good standing.
