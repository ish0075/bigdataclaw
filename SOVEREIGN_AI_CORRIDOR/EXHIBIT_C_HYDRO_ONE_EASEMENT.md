### Easement Rights Granted

1. **Construct, operate, and maintain** 230 kV transmission lines and associated facilities
2. **Access** the easement lands for inspection, repair, and maintenance
3. **Clear vegetation** and maintain safety corridors
4. **Restrict certain activities** within the easement zone for safety
5. **Exercise other rights** customary for high-voltage transmission easements

## PART 4: COORDINATION REQUIREMENTS

### Hydro One Contact Requirements

The Licence Agreement requires coordination with Hydro One regarding:

1. **Safety clearances** for any work near energized lines
2. **Access protocols** for Hydro One maintenance activities
3. **Notification requirements** for ground disturbance
4. **Emergency procedures** for incidents involving transmission infrastructure

### Licence Obligations

Per Article 5 of the Licence Agreement:
- Licensee shall not interfere with Hydro One's operation, maintenance, or safety rights
- Licensee shall comply with all Hydro One safety requirements
- Licensee shall coordinate all site activities with Hydro One as required
