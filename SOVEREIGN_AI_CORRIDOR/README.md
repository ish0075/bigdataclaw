- ✅ **$1/year rent** - No Crown cost
- ✅ **No construction** until Hydro One confirms
- ✅ **12+12 month term** - 24 months to validate
- ✅ **Exclusivity** - Crown can't grant competing uses

- **Now:** TS verbal confirmation documented ("guy in the truck")

- **Protection:** No security deposit, no construction until confirmation

- Hydro One transmission = infrastructure, not natural resources
