## COMMON IO QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

**Q: "Why $1 rent?"**
A: Exploratory phase only. All construction and major payments deferred until Hydro One confirms interconnection feasibility. Zero cost to Crown if project doesn't proceed.

**Q: "What if Hydro One doesn't confirm?"**
A: Licence terminates or converts to standard Crown land use. Crown bears no obligation. Site returns to available status.

**Q: "Is this an end-run around proper process?"**
A: No. This IS the proper process for strategic infrastructure - phased development with validation gates. Used for major projects across Ontario.

**Q: "Why not MNRF?"**
A: Infrastructure Ontario handles Crown land development for strategic economic infrastructure. This project has ISED Federal Lands Priority Access designation and involves Hydro One transmission coordination.

**Q: "What activities are permitted?"**
A: Environmental testing, geotechnical surveys, borings - non-constructive exploratory activities only. No buildings, no grade alteration, no permanent infrastructure.
