# Operations Team

The Operations Team manages relationships, communications, and deal execution support.

## Mission

Build and maintain relationships with capital sources, builders, and brokers to convert opportunities into closed deals.

## Structure

**Reports to:** CMO

**Team Members:**
- Outreach Strategist (communications)
- Market Researcher (market intelligence)

## Key Processes

### Daily
- Outreach queue processing
- Response handling
- Market data updates

### Weekly
- Outreach metrics review
- Relationship health check
- Content/insight creation

### Monthly
- Relationship pipeline review
- Strategy refinement
- New market preparation

## Success Metrics

- Response rate: >25%
- Meeting conversion: >10%
- Relationship depth: 4+ touchpoints/quarter
- Content quality score: >80%
