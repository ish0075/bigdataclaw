# Acquisition Team

The Acquisition Team is responsible for finding and qualifying commercial real estate investment opportunities.

## Mission

Maintain a pipeline of 50+ qualified opportunities and acquire $100M in assets within 12 months.

## Structure

**Reports to:** CTO

**Team Members:**
- Buyer Scout (hot money identification)
- Capital Analyst (capital source research)
- Transaction Scout (deal flow monitoring)

## Key Processes

### Daily
- Hot money scan (Buyer Scout)
- Transaction monitoring (Transaction Scout)
- Pipeline status updates

### Weekly
- Pipeline review with CTO
- Research requests from CMO team
- Market updates

### Monthly
- Full pipeline analysis
- Strategy review
- Coverage gap assessment

## Success Metrics

- Pipeline value: $100M+ target
- New opportunities: 20+/week
- Lead quality: >70% score rate
- Research turnaround: 48 hours
