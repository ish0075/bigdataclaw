# Monthly Market Report

## Objective

Provide comprehensive market intelligence on target markets and asset classes.

## Contents

1. **Market Overview**
   - Vacancy rates
   - Rent/sale trends
   - Absorption rates
   - Construction pipeline

2. **Submarket Analysis**
   - Performance by submarket
   - Emerging areas
   - Declining areas

3. **Competitive Activity**
   - Recent transactions
   - New entrants
   - Pricing trends

4. **Forecast**
   - Market outlook
   - Risk factors
   - Opportunity areas

## Output

- Written report (5-10 pages)
- Data tables
- Charts/visuals
- Executive summary

## Frequency

1st of each month

## Distribution

- CEO
- CTO
- CMO
