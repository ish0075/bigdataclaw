# Quarterly Strategy Review

## Objective

Assess progress toward $100M acquisition goal and adjust strategy as needed.

## Agenda

1. **Goal Progress** (20 min)
   - Acquisitions closed
   - Pipeline status
   - IRR achieved
   - Budget utilization

2. **Performance Analysis** (20 min)
   - Conversion rates
   - Deal velocity
   - Team productivity
   - Market coverage

3. **Market Assessment** (20 min)
   - Market conditions
   - Competitive landscape
   - Emerging opportunities
   - Risk factors

4. **Strategy Adjustment** (30 min)
   - Goal revision (if needed)
   - Resource reallocation
   - Market prioritization
   - Team changes

5. **Next Quarter Plan** (10 min)
   - Targets
   - Key initiatives
   - Resource needs

## Output

- Quarterly review report
- Updated strategy document
- Revised goals (if applicable)
- Resource requests

## Frequency

First week of each quarter

## Attendees

- CEO (lead)
- CTO
- CMO
- Board (as appropriate)
