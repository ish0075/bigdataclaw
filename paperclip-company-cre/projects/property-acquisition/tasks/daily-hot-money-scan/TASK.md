# Daily Hot Money Scan

## Objective

Identify and score new hot money leads daily to maintain a fresh pipeline of capital sources.

## Steps

1. **Query NERVE**: Get all hot money leads from last 24 hours
2. **Score Each Lead**: Apply scoring framework (cash, fit, geography, recency)
3. **Research High-Value**: Deep dive on leads scoring >70
4. **Create Alerts**: Generate briefs for CTO review
5. **Log Results**: Record scan metrics

## Output

- Scored lead list
- Briefs for leads >70 score
- Daily scan summary

## Frequency

Daily at 9:00 AM ET

## Success Criteria

- 100% of new leads scored within 24 hours
- All >70 leads flagged for review
- Zero data gaps
