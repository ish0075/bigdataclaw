# Hot Money Analysis Skill

Systematic approach to analyzing hot money leads and capital sources.

## Lead Scoring Framework

### Score 1-100 Based On:

**Cash Amount (30 points)**
- $50M+: 30 pts
- $25-50M: 25 pts
- $10-25M: 20 pts
- $5-10M: 15 pts
- <$5M: 10 pts

**Asset Class Fit (25 points)**
- Exact match to our targets: 25 pts
- Adjacent/acceptable: 15 pts
- Outside focus: 5 pts

**Geography (25 points)**
- Primary market: 25 pts
- Secondary market: 15 pts
- New/unproven market: 5 pts

**Recency (20 points)**
- < 7 days: 20 pts
- 7-30 days: 15 pts
- 30-90 days: 10 pts
- > 90 days: 5 pts

### Scoring Tiers

- **90-100**: Immediate priority — alert CEO/CMO
- **75-89**: High value — process within 24 hours
- **60-74**: Medium — batch process weekly
- **< 60**: Low priority — monitor only

## Research Checklist

For each lead scoring >75:

### Step 1: Entity Research
- [ ] Legal entity name
- [ ] Business type (family office, REIT, developer, etc.)
- [ ] Location/HQ
- [ ] Website and public info

### Step 2: Transaction History
- [ ] Past deals (use NERVE)
- [ ] Deal sizes and types
- [ ] Markets of focus
- [ ] Partners/joint venture history

### Step 3: Decision Makers
- [ ] Identify key personnel
- [ ] LinkedIn profiles
- [ ] Contact info (if available)
- [ ] Background check

### Step 4: Fit Analysis
- [ ] Asset class alignment
- [ ] Market overlap
- [ ] Deal size match
- [ ] Timing/urgency indicators

### Step 5: Relationship Path
- [ ] Mutual connections
- [ ] Prior interactions with us
- [ ] Conference/event overlap
- [ ] Warm intro opportunities

## Output: Capital Source Brief

```markdown
## Capital Source Brief: [Entity Name]

**Score:** [X]/100 ([Tier])

**Entity Profile:**
- Name: [Legal name]
- Type: [Family Office/REIT/etc]
- Location: [HQ]
- Contact: [Key person]

**Capital:**
- Available: $[Amount]
- Timeline: [Urgency]
- Asset Class: [Type]

**History:**
- [Deal 1]
- [Deal 2]
- [Deal 3]

**Fit Assessment:**
- Strengths: [Why they're a good target]
- Concerns: [Any red flags]
- Approach: [Recommended strategy]

**Next Action:**
- [Specific next step]
- Owner: [Who handles]
- Timeline: [When]
```

## Red Flags

Watch for and escalate:
- Litigation history
- Failed deals/no closes
- Unrealistic expectations
- Bad reputation in market
- Unclear capital source

## Follow-up cadence

**Tier 1 (90-100):**
- Day 0: Alert CEO/CMO
- Day 1: Research complete
- Day 2: Outreach drafted
- Day 3: First contact

**Tier 2 (75-89):**
- Day 0: Create brief
- Day 1: Assign to outreach
- Day 3: First contact
- Weekly: Follow-up
