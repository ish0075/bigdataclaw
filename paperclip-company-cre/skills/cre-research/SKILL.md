# CRE Research Skill

Standardized methodology for commercial real estate research and analysis.

## Market Analysis Framework

### 1. Market Overview

**Gather:**
- Total inventory (SF or units)
- Vacancy rate
- Absorption rate (SF/units per quarter)
- Average rent/price
- Construction pipeline

**Sources:**
- NERVE market data
- CoStar (if available)
- Local market reports

### 2. Submarket Analysis

**Segments to analyze:**
- Class A/B/C breakdown
- Geographic submarkets
- Product type variations

**Key metrics:**
- Rent/sale price by segment
- Vacancy by segment
- Concession packages
- Lease terms

### 3. Competitive Landscape

**Identify:**
- Top 5 competitors
- Their positioning
- Recent transactions
- Market share

## Property Valuation Approach

### Comparable Sales Method

**Step 1: Find Comps**
- Same asset class
- Similar size (+/- 20%)
- Same/ adjacent submarket
- Recent sales (6-12 months)

**Step 2: Adjust**
- Size adjustments
- Quality/condition
- Location premium/discount
- Timing (market changes)

**Step 3: Apply**
- $/SF or cap rate
- Cross-check with other methods

### Income Approach

**NOI Calculation:**
```
Gross Potential Rent
- Vacancy (market rate)
+ Other Income
= Effective Gross Income
- Operating Expenses
= NOI
```

**Cap Rate Application:**
```
Value = NOI / Cap Rate
```

Use market cap rates from comparable sales.

### Replacement Cost

**Estimate:**
- Land value
- Construction cost per SF
- Soft costs (15-20%)
- Developer profit (15-20%)

Use for new construction or special use.
