# Outreach Drafting Skill

Guidelines for drafting effective CRE outreach messages.

## The 3-30-3 Rule

**3 seconds**: Subject line gets attention
**30 seconds**: First paragraph delivers value  
**3 minutes**: Full read worth their time

## Message Structure

### Subject Line Formulas

**For Hot Money:**
- "Your $[X]M [Asset Class] search — [Market] opportunity"
- "Saw you closed on [Property] — similar deal in [Market]"
- "[Asset Class] in [Market] at [Cap Rate]% cap"

**For Builders:**
- "[Market] construction partner for [Asset Class]?"
- "[X] unit [Asset Class] deal — development partner?"
- "Saw [Project] — similar opportunity"

**For Follow-up:**
- "Quick follow: [Original subject]"
- "One more thing on [Topic]"
- "[Name], one week follow-up"

### Email Structure

**Opening (Hook):**
- Reference their recent activity
- Show you did your homework
- Be specific, not generic

**BAD:** "I saw you're looking for deals"
**GOOD:** "Saw you closed on the 200-unit Oakbrook multifamily in Q2"

**Value (Body):**
- Lead with insight they don't have
- Be specific about opportunity
- Show relevance to their criteria

**Ask (CTA):**
- Low friction next step
- Specific time commitment
- Clear value exchange

**Examples:**
- "Worth a 15-min call?"
- "Can I send you the teaser?"
- "Are you in [City] next week?"

**Credibility:**
- Brief NERVE credentials
- Social proof (deals done)
- Professional signature

## Templates

```
We have a 200-unit value-add in East York at a 5.2% cap — below your typical basis but similar upside profile to Midtown. Fully occupied, in-place rents 15% below market.

Worth a 15-min call this week?

Best,
[Name]
NERVE Capital Partners
[Phone] | [Email]
```

## Personalization Guide

**Always customize:**
- [ ] Their name (spelled correctly)
- [ ] Recent deal or activity
- [ ] Their specific criteria/mandate
- [ ] Market references
- [ ] Ask appropriate to relationship stage

**NEVER:**
- Use "Dear Sir/Madam"
- Send generic templates
- Ask for "just 15 minutes of your time" (everyone does)
- Overwhelm with attachments
- Use ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!

## Response Handling

**Positive response:**
- Escalate to CMO immediately
- Draft reply within 2 hours
- Propose specific next steps

**Negative response:**
- Thank them
- Ask what IS a fit
- Note preference for future

**No response:**
- Continue follow-up sequence
- Track for future campaigns
- Try different angle after 30 days

## A/B Testing

Track and test:
- Subject line open rates
- CTA click rates
- Length (short vs. detailed)
- Personalization level
- Timing (day of week, time of day)

Report results to CMO weekly.
