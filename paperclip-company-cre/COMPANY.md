# NERVE Capital Partners

## Mission

We are an AI-powered commercial real estate investment company that combines data intelligence with relationship-driven deal making to acquire high-quality assets at attractive valuations.

## Strategy

1. **Data-Driven Sourcing**: Use NERVE platform to identify hot money, portfolio matches, and off-market opportunities
2. **Relationship Building**: Maintain active dialogue with capital sources, builders, and brokers
3. **Rapid Analysis**: Deploy AI agents for quick underwriting and market analysis
4. **Systematic Outreach**: Personalized, high-conversion outreach to decision-makers

## Operating Principles

- Speed matters: First mover advantage on off-market deals
- Relationships are everything: Build trust before you need it
- Data beats gut: Every decision backed by NERVE intelligence
- Follow-up wins: Persistence without annoyance

## Company Structure

```
CEO (Strategy & Capital Allocation)
├── CTO (Technology & Data)
│   ├── Buyer Scout (Hot money & buyer analysis)
│   ├── Builder Scout (Construction partners)
│   └── Market Researcher (Market intelligence)
├── CMO (Relationships & Outreach)
│   ├── Capital Analyst (Capital sources & research)
│   ├── Outreach Strategist (Communication & follow-up)
│   └── Transaction Scout (Deal flow & opportunities)
└── Operations (Execution)
```

## Key Metrics

- Pipeline: 50+ active opportunities
- Conversion: 10% LOI to close
- Response Rate: 25%+ on outreach
- Deal Velocity: 90 days from intro to LOI
