### THE PROBLEM
Commercial real estate professionals spend **4-6 hours per property** on research:
- Manual data collection from 10+ sources
- No centralized intelligence platform
- Missed opportunities due to slow speed
- Expensive analyst teams ($240K/year)

**Result:** Deals are lost to faster competitors.

### THE SOLUTION
**32 AI agents** working 24/7:
- **Hot Money Radar:** Identifies capital-rich sellers in 24 hours
- **6-Phase Research:** 10 minutes vs 4-6 hour industry standard
- **96K Agent Database:** Instant recruitment capability
- **NERVE Platform:** Real-time agent orchestration

**Result:** 36x faster, 3 FTEs replaced, 2 extra deals/quarter.
